A **false red** cannot hide a defect the way a false green can; what it costs is a diagnosis, and here it cost one. And the operational lesson is small and concrete: **do not `pkill` the net to re-run it after an edit** — let it finish, or accept that the next run's cache rows and anything downstream of the FFI probe may lie. The reason the runs were killed at all was CLAUDE.md's own rule that the suites are run *after the last edit rather than after the last interesting edit*; obeying it by killing is the wrong way to obey it, and waiting is the right one
