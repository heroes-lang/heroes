## What closed it

**Panel 165**,
`docs/panel/165-the-check-it-would-add-was-not-one-and-the-route-that-shipped-had-none.md`,
convened 2026-09-19 with six briefs written to disk and the tree frozen from the
briefs going out until the synthesis. The item asked that the sitting carry
measurement 035's numbers rather than an estimate, and it did: the census table,
the `L_tmpnam` divergence and the Principle 0 count are in the shared brief with
the commands that produced them.

**Route 6 is refused**, on the compiler-engineer's and the spec-warden's vetoes.
The sentence it rested on — *"the only route of the seven where the compiler
CHECKS the extent"* — was falsified three times with three instruments, and the
inversion is that route 4 already checks a field's extent **against the header**
while route 6 would check the author against the author.

**What the sitting found underneath it is larger than what it was convened on**,
which is the second time running that has happened here: three defects, filed
063, 064 and 065, on a register that read `OPEN: 0`. One of them is memory
corruption at exit 0 in code shipped eight commits earlier.

## What it leaves

- **Routes 12, 13 and 14** are recorded with their measurements so they are not
  rediscovered as new. Route 12 — a header `constant` as the extent — is the one
  that dissolves this milestone's own headline: a declared extent cannot be
  portable as a **literal**, and as a **name** it can, because the macro expands
  in the emitted C against the real header. The wall is the Heroes grammar.
- **`panel 165` is queued in `docs/work/DECIDE.md`** for the author's
  ratification, with the conservative resolution recorded beside it.
- **Defects 063 and 065 are open**, and panel 166 was convened on them the same
  day. 064 closed on 2026-09-19.

## And the item's own premise held

It asked for the numbers to travel rather than an estimate, because panel 164 had
queued the route precisely for want of them. They travelled — and five of the six
briefs still carried a coordinator's error that a seat caught. The instrument the
item named was the right one; what it could not ask for is the thing this project
keeps relearning, that **nothing checks the brief against the world**.
