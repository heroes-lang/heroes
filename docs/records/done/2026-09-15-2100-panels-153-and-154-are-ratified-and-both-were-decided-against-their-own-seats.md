    **Both were decided AGAINST their own seats' convergence, by the same
    instrument, for the same reason.** At 153 four of five seats adopted a route
    with a new built-in and a spec sentence; at 154 four of five adopted a route
    with a fourth parameter word. In both the completeness critic measured an
    option nobody had listed, and in both the option cost nothing a reader must
    learn:

    | sitting | what the seats converged on | what the critic measured, and what was adopted |
    |---|---|---|
    | **153** | a `read()` built-in and a widened rule, +77 real | three lines of a header of the author's own, and the SHIPPED compiler reads the struct and walks the list, output byte-identical to the equivalent C |

    **The tell is the same sentence in both**, and the 154 critic wrote it: *"here
    the cheapest in tokens and the most complete are the same route, which is the
    tell that the frame, not the price, decided."* Five seats differentiated by
    input still share one blind spot, which is what the critic exists for — and at
    153 the blind spot was that every seat searched inside the compiler, where
    §1.11 says nothing in this language comes from.

    **What the two yeses settle.** That `one_tag_one_type` admits one handle and
    one record with fields per tag, which closed defect 042. That
    `-fno-delete-null-pointer-checks` is in the flag list, which closed defect 045
    and, with it, two deterministic wrong answers at exit 0. That the null window
    is the platform's own floor rather than a page. And that routes C and D of
    panel 154 stay refused, D because §1.12 refuses a provenance rule in advance
    in words no seat had quoted.

    **WHAT IS OWED, with the trigger that releases each, so none is lost.**

    - **Route A's guard**, adopted and unlanded: it buys the blame line and not
      the class. Its patch exists, formatted and passing `canonical`, `layout` and
      `order`. It lands when a milestone next opens `selfhost/emit/ops.hero`.
    - **Route B's fourth word**, deferred on the rule the sitting was held to:
      *two is a class and one is a witness*. Exactly one shipped program would be
      refused by the guard today.
    - **What § 13 says about reaching a struct through a header of the author's
      own**, which panel 153's R2 leaves: a route the specification does not
      mention is a route a reader of the prompt cannot find, and that was this
      sitting's own argument against the header route. It is spec text and
      therefore priced; panel 154's critic measured an unspent removal, **R4 at
      −9 real**, which is the obvious payment. It waits for the milestone that
      next opens `spec/`.
    - **The 64 KiB floor off Darwin**, which is the smaller claim rather than a
      measurement: `.claude/rules/platforms.md` says a platform fact is run on a
      platform, and the Linux and Windows floors were not. The falsifier is a
      program that reads a field past it and dies at 139 in silence.

    154 built that guard the same evening**, as a compile flag, and closed defect

    sitting is owed on it — only the spec sentence above.
