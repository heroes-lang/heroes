2026-08-11 | **Polymorphic recursion is refused by the monomorphisation pass**, not by the checker and not by a depth limit (panel 029 R4). It type-checks clean at exit 0 today and design.md does not mention it anywhere. The checker cannot see it — it checks a generic body once, polymorphically, and never expands it; a depth limit would exit 2, *"the compiler could not run"*, for a program §4.12 makes legal. So the pass refuses structurally: instantiating `f` at `S` from within `f` at `T` where `S` **properly contains** `T` is unbounded, decidable, and exactly MLton's finiteness argument — its whole-program monomorphiser has been total for twenty-five years *because* SML bans this.
