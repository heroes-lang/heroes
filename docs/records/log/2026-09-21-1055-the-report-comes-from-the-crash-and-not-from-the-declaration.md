## What the brief got wrong, and who found it

The coordinator's shared brief put three routes on the table, all about the
vocabulary, and listed what was not on the ballot without naming design.md
Part 6's row :2667, ratified six days earlier, which refuses exactly the form
routes A and B propose. **The spec-warden found it.** The brief also offered
`borrows` as a keeps-word; **the historian found** that in every shipping
precedent that word means *does not keep*. And the brief treated the defect's
empty stderr as a message going missing; **the critic measured** that on this
platform the allocator writes nothing for any bad free, and that 133 and 134
are SIGTRAP and SIGABRT, two paths and not randomness.

## Why a report and not a word

Three of the seven measured shapes of the defect free the leased bytes through
a function that has no pointer parameter: a callback handed as a value, a later
call that frees what an earlier one stashed, an out-cell. No word written on a
parameter reaches them. The runtime's counter of live leases reaches every one
where a lease is live, from the signal the allocator raises, and it costs no
spec token, no grammar slot, and no claim about C that the compiler cannot
check. The word would have been a claim: a wrong type is exit 1 with the header
as judge, a wrong ownership word is exit 0 with no judge, measured.
