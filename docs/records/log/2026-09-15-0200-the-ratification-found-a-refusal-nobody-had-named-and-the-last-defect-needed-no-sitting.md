# The ratification found a refusal nobody had named, and the last defect needed no sitting

2026-09-15, 02:00. Two decisions and one repair, in one session, after panel
149's work had already shipped.

## Panel 149 is ratified as adopted

The robust resolution, not the conservative one. The alternative recorded under
CL-040 — R1 producer-side only, the consumer side at one level, no releaser
check — is declined.

**The order is what made the verdict worth having.** It came after the repair
shipped, as panel 148's did, so it judged what LANDED rather than what was
proposed, and found three things a verdict on a proposal could not.

**The spec sentence the resolution bought has not landed.** R1 was priced at
**+4 real** and it is unwritten: `heroes measure --refresh` needs
`ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, unset on this machine, and the instrument refuses a verdict
against a stale count — *"that is the sentence that spent 998 tokens nobody
had"*. The draft was written, measured at +12 on the vendored ranks for a
phrasing longer than the warden's, and reverted, because a spec change that
cannot update its own recorded count takes four suite checks red with it. **A
ratification does not land a sentence**, and saying so is the point of writing it
down rather than letting the yes imply it.

**The resolution understated what shipped.** R3 was adopted as *no refusal
lands*, and `unread_releaser` landed beside it: the ffi-pragmatist's condition,
promoted. It closes a hole three measurements found at exit 0 — `acquires
sqlite3_notafunction`, and `acquires sqlite3_finalize` on a `Db`. A sitting that
adopts *no refusal* and lands one should say so rather than leave a reader to
find it in the code.

**And the critic's structural finding was the whole sitting.** Five seats
differentiated by input still shared one blind spot: every one of them read the
rule as a question about PRODUCERS. The consumer side was in no brief, the
coordinator's own included, and a producer-only widening was measured to stay
silent on raylib's `Font` — the only real shipped-library case — while erroring
on the contrived one. **The repair would have shipped looking correct and doing
nothing.**

## Defect 036 closes, and it needed no sitting

`owned <freer>` after a non-`cstr` result checked at exit 0 and made clang blame
the compiler at exit 2. **The refusal already existed and watched one of its two
positions**: `owned_needs_cstr` has asked a marked PARAMETER its type since
2026-09-13, and `mark_refusals` never asked it of the result.

**So CLAUDE.md § 4 does not reach it.** Widening a diagnostic CLASS goes to the
panel; this class was already decided, already written, and already shipping at
the other position. The repair is one call site and a generalised parameter.

**Three documents agreed and only the rule disagreed.** `spec § 13` gives the
mark after *"a `cstr` result or a `char **` out-parameter"*, and the diagnostic's
own note has always read *"it belongs on a `@x: cstr` out-parameter or on a
`cstr` result"*. The rule asked one of the two.

## Two things the sitting named and nobody filed, now filed

**037**, the `ptr` blind spot, which panel 149's ffi-pragmatist called the more
serious of the two holes it looked at: where a group consumes a `ptr`, nothing
demands a mark on the call handing one back, so the program leaks at exit 0 with
an empty stderr. The runtime half already works — `acquires` on a `ptr` counts,
measured — and only the static demand is missing. It outranks what was repaired
here, because that hole was loud and this one is silent (§1.12).

**038**, the per-element release: `Slot[4]` behind one mark, released element by
element, aborts at 134, and the runtime message names two causes and neither
fits. It was not covered by the old three-cause wording either, whose third cause
was an UNMARKED producer, so nothing was lost when that clause was struck and
nothing has ever named this shape. **The route nobody listed** is the critic's:
all three candidates act on the producer's declaration, and the two programs that
disagree differ on the consumer's.
