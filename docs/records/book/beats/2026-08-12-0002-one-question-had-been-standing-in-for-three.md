2026-08-12 | M8a | One question had been standing in for three since the strings milestone, and nobody could see it while there were only two files. When the compiler wanted to know whether a piece of text belonged to the small built-in library, it was really asking three different things: may an error message point here, may the generated code claim the author's file for it, and which name should the linker see. With two files those have one answer. With four they do not — and the difference decides whether running a project's tests runs the tests in all of its files or silently only those in the one you named. The split cost two words and would have cost a whole class of missing tests.
