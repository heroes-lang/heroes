# M-launch-posts — the first posts, each with the numbers its command produced

**Scheduled 2026-10-14, at row 86, by the author's instruction**, immediately
after the gate: the author chose that the first posts follow it. It is the end
the author named for the chain, meant as: *to reach a point where I can
promote the language, with blog posts on the aggregators and on social
media*. No warrant is claimed.

**What it delivers.** Three or four posts, each with numbers a command
produced: the control arm's measured difference in a title rather than the
slogan alone; the FFI over sqlite and curl with no shim; the compiler that
compiles itself; the method. The first hour a stranger spends tried before
the first post, by paid readers and by a couple of people. A page of
measured numbers. The answers to the objections anyone can predict, read from
the site's `why/not` pages. Where to publish, and in what order. Every
publication is an outward act asked for each time, and the social accounts
are the author's.

**What it inherits.** A project whose compiler an AI assistant writes says so
on its first screen: until 2026-10-14 only the site's about page did
(`site/src/html/about.html`), and the README's first screen changed in the
commit that scheduled this row. Part of the community these posts reach
refuses LLM contributions outright: Zig's code of conduct carries a *Strict No
LLM / No AI Policy* (https://ziglang.org/code-of-conduct/, read 2026-10-14). A
disclosure read on the first screen is the experiment; the same fact found by
a reader looks like something hidden.

**Named for the deliverable**, the posts. Not `M-public-launch`, the act; not
`M-blog-posts`, the medium; not `M-first-readers`, the audience.
