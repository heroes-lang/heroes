A switch of stacks C makes changes no value a function holds, wherever the stacks lie. Where C leaves a callback's frames without their returning (by a jump, an exception or a coroutine it never resumes, say), a value they held of more than 4,096 bytes, or holding a `partial` record, and a buffer they lent to C stay held until the program ends, counted with the live ones against 1 GiB for the whole program, past which it aborts, saying how many callbacks have not returned.
