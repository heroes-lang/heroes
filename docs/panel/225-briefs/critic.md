# Panel 225, completeness critic

Two passes. **First pass, before any seat starts**: read `00-shared.md` and the two seats' briefs
and run every framing fact they state (the line numbers and counts, the quoted sentences of
`selfhost/` and design.md, the reproducers' readings, the two filings' and the tag's times, the
lane's HEAD); return the ones that do not hold or that you could not verify, and anything a brief
asserts without its command. Write it to `docs/panel/225-reports/critic-briefs.md` in the lane.
**Second pass, after the seats**: read their reports and name what is MISSING: a route nobody
listed, a claim asserted and not measured, a contradiction between seats with which side is
checkable, and the question the sitting should have asked. Write it to
`docs/panel/225-reports/critic.md`. You give no verdict. Same rules as the seats: your own copy,
`.claude/worktrees/225-critic/` made from `7b97b9438`, if you run anything; no paid run; no
credential printed.
