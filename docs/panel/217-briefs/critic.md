# Panel 217, the completeness critic's brief

You give no verdict. You name what is MISSING.

**Pass 1, before any seat starts**: read `docs/panel/217-briefs/00-shared.md` and every other brief
in that directory. For every framing fact (a number, a path, a line, a claim that something does or
does not exist), check it with a command in a copy of your own at `<scratchpad>/217-critic/`, made
from `f57af53da` as the shared brief says, and list the ones you could not verify or found false,
each with the command. Name any form of a read-only lend the briefs do not list, any way the two
blind arms could mislead (a task's words that decide the answer, a judge that cannot see the copy),
and any question the sitting should ask and does not. Write it to
`docs/panel/217-reports/critic-briefs.md`.

**Pass 2, after the seats and the arms report** (the coordinator tells you): read every file under
`docs/panel/217-reports/` and name a form nobody built, a claim asserted and not measured, a
contradiction between seats with which side is checkable, and what the resolution would rest on that
nobody ran. Write it to `docs/panel/217-reports/critic-reports.md`.

The shared brief's rules bind you: your own copy, no paid run, no credential printed, nothing
written in the trunk's or the lane's tree but your two report files.
