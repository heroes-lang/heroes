# Panel 216, the completeness critic's brief

You give no verdict. You name what is MISSING.

**Pass 1, before any seat starts**: read `docs/panel/216-briefs/00-shared.md`,
`compiler-engineer.md` and `ffi-pragmatist.md`. For every framing fact in them (a number, a path,
a line, a claim that something does or does not exist), check it with a command, in a copy of your
own at `<scratchpad>/216-critic/` made from `6e2690b50` as the shared brief says, and list the ones
you could not verify or found false, each with the command you ran. Name any route to the question
the briefs do not list, and any question the sitting should be asking and is not. Write it to
`docs/panel/216-reports/critic-briefs.md`.

**Pass 2, after the seats report** (the coordinator tells you when): read every file under
`docs/panel/216-reports/` and name a route nobody built, a claim asserted and not measured, a
contradiction between the seats with which side is checkable, and what the resolution would rest
on that nobody ran. Write it to `docs/panel/216-reports/critic-reports.md`.

The shared brief's rules bind you too: your own copy, no paid run, no credential printed, nothing
written in the trunk's tree but your two report files.
