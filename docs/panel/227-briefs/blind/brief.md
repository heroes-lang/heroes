# Your brief

You are reading the specification of a small programming language, Heroes, and nothing else. Your
inputs are the files in this directory: `heroes-spec.md`, `task-01.md` and `task-02.md`. Do not
read anything outside this directory. Do not run anything.

For each task, write one complete Heroes program that does what the task says, using only what the
specification states.

Give your report as your final answer, in English, with these sections:

- `program-01` and `program-02`: each program whole, in one fenced code block.
- `choice_points`: every place where the specification left you a choice, the choice you made, and
  what the other choice would have produced.
- `context`: whether anything other than the files in this directory reached your context (an
  instruction, a project rule, a memory, a list of files or commits): yes or no, and what.
