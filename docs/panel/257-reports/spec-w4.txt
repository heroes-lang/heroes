A callback is a **parameter**, never a result or a signal handler, nor called by one, nor by C on a thread where one of its signal handlers has not ended, which aborts the program, saying the handler was not seen to end; a handler ends by its return or by a `longjmp`, `siglongjmp`, `_longjmp` or `setcontext` out of it, but not by an inline jump, a `swapcontext` away from it or a jump onto another stack given to `makecontext`; its parameters follow the same rule
and `()` is `void`: `atexit(f: (function() -> ())) -> i32`.
