A callback called after a handler of C's left by a jump no function of the C library makes (written in assembly, say), by a `swapcontext` away from it, by a jump onto a stack `makecontext` made, or by a jump that lands within it may abort, saying the handler was not seen to end; a handler that returns, or that C leaves by `longjmp`, `siglongjmp`, `_longjmp` or `setcontext` onto the thread's own stack, is over, and its callbacks run.
