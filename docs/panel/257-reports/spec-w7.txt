A handler of C's ends by its return or by a `longjmp`, `siglongjmp`, `_longjmp` or `setcontext` out of it; left any other way (by an inline jump, a `swapcontext` away from it or a jump onto another stack given to `makecontext`) it is still taken to be running, and a callback called later on its thread from deeper in the stack than the handler was may abort, saying the handler was not seen to end.
