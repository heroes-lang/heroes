A callback called after C left a handler of C's, a signal handler on its thread, by a jump no function of the C library makes (written in assembly, say), by a `swapcontext` away from it, by a jump or a `setcontext` onto a stack once given to `makecontext` or into a coroutine the signal did not interrupt, or by a jump into the handler's own frames, may abort, saying the handler was not seen to end, or that C called it from inside one. On a thread where no signal handler of C's has run (counting a signal with `hero_signal_watch` runs none), no coroutine switch or jump of C's makes a callback abort in these words.
