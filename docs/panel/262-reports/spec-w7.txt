A signal handler of C's ends, for the callbacks of its thread, when it returns, and, on Linux with glibc on 64-bit Arm or x86 and on a Mac with Apple silicon, when C jumps out of it by `longjmp`, `siglongjmp`, `_longjmp` or a `setcontext` to a context `getcontext` took, back to a call the signal interrupted; elsewhere (an Intel Mac, another C library or system, Windows), and after C left it any other way, a callback called on that thread may abort, saying the handler was not seen to end, or that C called it from inside one.
