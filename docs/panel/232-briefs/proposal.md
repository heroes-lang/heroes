# Panel 232: what `1.0.0` will promise, said before it is promised

M-compatibility-promise, the sitting the author approved on 2026-10-29 (the
full panel, the blind seat within 12 USD). Not a retro-record: every item is
open. Repaired after the critic's first pass with the briefs
(`docs/panel/232-reports/critic-briefs.md`).

**A frame no seat argues past** (the author's rule of 2026-10-29, CLAUDE.md
§ 2): before `1.0.0` no form enters to keep an older program compiling. Each
item is a sentence that says what the language already does, a sentence the
compiler does not keep and one of the two repaired, or a promise about
`1.0.0` itself.

1. **The order of evaluation** (decision 2217's *A*). One true sentence, or
   as few as stay true, for what the compiler does: an expression's parts
   left to right as written, a write's two phases, the built-ins that call a
   function value; and whether § 9 owes a direct call's labels the declared
   order.
2. **`<<` and `>>`** (task 2245, 1): § 7 states what design.md § 4.14
   rules, the wrap and the sign copied; or an abort on a lost set bit, which
   amends § 4.14 and answers its reason.
3. **`==` on a copy of a handle given back** (task 2245, 2): § 13's clause
   and the runtime made to agree, one way or the other.
4. **A stack exhausted without recursion** (task 2245, 3): *a call chain
   deeper than the stack aborts*, tested on the shapes beside it first.
5. **§ 11's `exit`** (task 2250): whether the sentence says a literal is
   refused, and what of its edges.
6. **The paragraph `1.0.0` rests on** (task 1066): its words, its home, what
   it excludes, whether it promises one way, and the deployment model.
7. **Whether `1.0.0` promises a diagnostic's code**, and not its words.
8. **The compatibility suite's corpus** from `v1.0.0`.
