# SCHEDULED — work that already has a milestone

Neither a question nor a decision: work with a **home**, waiting for it. Nothing
here needs the author, and nothing here should be done early — each item names
the milestone that will read it, and doing it sooner means doing it against a
smaller corpus, a smaller compiler, or a measurement not yet taken.

`/step` reads this when it opens the milestone named in the item — its § 1
Orient says so, and that line is the whole mechanism. **There is deliberately no
`/scheduled` skill**: a third verb reading a list `/step` already has to open
answers no question the other two do not, which is CLAUDE.md §10's stopping rule
applied to the skills instead of the CLI. A list nobody is *obliged* to open is
write-only, and the obligation is cheaper than a command.

**Only open items live here.** The moment one is done it is ticked with what
closed it and moved to `docs/work/DONE.md`. Eleven ticked items had accumulated
here by 2026-08-26, two of them ticked not because the work finished but because
the item had *moved elsewhere* — audit trail, which belongs in the record.

**If an item loses its home it moves to `docs/work/DECIDE.md`**, not here. Work
scheduled at nothing is a decision nobody has made. Two items left under this
rule on 2026-08-26: one named M-strings-ownership, closed 2026-08-05, and one
named no milestone at all.

**One notation: `- [ ]`.** No section headings, no prose sections.
