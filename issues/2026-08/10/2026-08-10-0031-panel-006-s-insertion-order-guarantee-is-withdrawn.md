2026-08-10 | **Panel 006's insertion-order guarantee is withdrawn as FALSE, not weakened.** `HeroMapHeader` has no order field, so arrival is **not stored** and no iteration strategy can recover it; and `cap` derives from a literal's **duplicate-inclusive** pair count, so two `==`-equal maps written differently iterate differently — measured: `{"a":1,"b":2,"c":3}` iterates `b a c` at cap 8, the same map with a duplicated first key iterates `b c a` at cap 16. So design.md's "map iteration follows insertion order" and the ROADMAP's "iteration order must be a function of the contents" were false from the day the map shipped. Panel 006's *argument* was sound and is answered rather than overruled: it feared unspecified order because a model's silent default is insertion order and Go's randomization is the recorded disaster — but that hazard needs a reader who iterates a map **directly**, and there is no such form. The fixed seed stays: determinism across runs is still a fixpoint requirement.
