# The author asks that the issues credit who got there first, and that a defect's number survive the move to GitHub

2026-10-11, written at 15:01 by the clock (`date`), on the trunk at `51e6bbb9`,
as M-issue-files step 9. Two instructions and one answer of 2026-10-07, given
on the coordinator's plan for the GitHub transcription, and the plan rewritten
on 2026-10-11 against the tree of that day and approved by the author. The
transcription itself is unchanged: the author's act, its verb a sitting's
question when they decide to transcribe (the *7a* of 2026-10-05).

**1. The issues credit whoever got there first.** Meant as: *when I create the
issues on GitHub I want them to speak, a template, the problem, the solution,
the commit; and where there were historical threads, or where I reconstructed
something, attribute the decision to the online reference or to the other
language's issue too; very honest in crediting whoever solved these things
before me.* The coordinator's first draft put a `## Prior art` on every issue
that closes and *not searched* where nothing was found; the author refused it
the same day, meant as: *not always; only where a sitting sat, the historian
spoke and what the historian said was taken into account; backwards too, in
those cases alone; elsewhere do not even write that the search was not made.*
**Settled**: a `decision` issue of such a sitting ends with `## Prior art`, one
line per precedent, `Taken from` or `Reached first by`, with its link and the
day it was re-read; nowhere else, and never a sentence about an absence
(`.claude/rules/records.md` § And the record says whose idea it was; CL-082).
Measured 2026-10-11: 7 of 2,010 issue files carried a link; 186 of 210
sittings' syntheses name the historian, 111 historian files, 36 with a source.

**2. A defect's number is its name, GitHub's an address.** Meant as: *sometimes
in the code we cite defect number so-and-so; let us not lose that when the
defects go to GitHub; a custom field, or something of the kind; think about
it.* Measured 2026-10-11: 6,925 citations of `defect NNN` under `selfhost/`,
`runtime/`, `seed/` and `tests/` on 560 numbers, 3,261 test files named after
one, 1,504 commit subjects; the register is the item line, `014` the one
number on two of them. **Settled**: the title opens `Defect NNN:`; the
organisation's issue field `Defect`, text, visible to everyone, holds the
numbers so `field.defect:NNN` finds the issue, created by an administrator,
the author's act; the twins of `014` name each other; the links between
bodies are a second pass; the verb asks the field before it creates. Refused:
rewriting the citations, reserving GitHub numbers, an autolink with a space
in its prefix, unverified (§ The issues; CL-083).

**3. What an issue says about its solution.** A `feature` or a `task` closes
under `## What was done`, one line and its commit; every repair or settling
section ends with its hash; a card's `self` is rendered as the hash of the
commit that created the file. The forms gain *Prior art* (decision) and *What
was done* (feature, task); the outsiders' forms do not change.

recommendation: what Heroes reached on its own and found reached before goes
on the thanks page too, in a group of its own in both editions, under that
page's rules; filed as the milestone's task, its push asked for.
