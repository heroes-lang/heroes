2026-09-23 | every seat of a sitting that builds works in its own
`<scratchpad>/<NNN>-<seat>/`, copied from the frozen tree at the HEAD the
briefs name, with its own compiler built from the seed there, and no seat reads,

## What it settles

`.claude/skills/panel/SKILL.md` said *`cp -r` the tree to the scratchpad and
work there*, and a session's scratchpad is shared with the seats it launches —
panel 170's completeness critic says so at line 226 of its report. So the
instruction put every seat in one place, and the rule that already binds a gate,
`.claude/rules/verification.md` § *The compiler that judges is a build
artifact*, did not reach a sitting.

## What it is not

It is not a panel ruling and it changes nothing a program sees. It adds one
paragraph to the skill's step 3, beside the two earlier times a copy alone was
not enough (panel 054's prototype in the repository, panel 056's `target/`
pointing back at it).
