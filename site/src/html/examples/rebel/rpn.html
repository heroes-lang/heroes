The reverse Polish calculator, written again with Rebel’s strings and numbers, its stack and its checks kept.

Rebel’s strings cuts an expression into its words, and its numbers reads a whole number and refuses any other text, or one too large for 64 bits, in place of the original’s own helpers. The stack stays this program’s, with its three mistakes, an operator with too few numbers under it, nothing to evaluate and numbers left over, and the check before a division: dividing a whole number by zero stops a Heroes program, so a calculator reading what a person types refuses a typed zero rather than end.
