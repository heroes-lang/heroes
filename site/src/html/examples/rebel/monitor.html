A system monitor with alerts and a terminal dashboard, in the family of htop, Netdata and supervisord, in small.

It runs the services its configuration names and keeps them running, restarting a failed one after a wait that doubles each time, in the order their dependencies give. It reads what their processes hold and spend, watches files and folders for changes, reads a sensor on a serial port, judges alert rules over every figure with thresholds and durations, runs a notifier when an alert fires or resolves, writes its journal and its services’ output to log files that rotate, exports metrics, traces and the journal over OTLP, the protocol of OpenTelemetry that monitoring tools read, answers other tools on a control socket, and draws its dashboard on the terminal.

The output on this page is its demo: services that are this program again, watched folders it edits, a thermometer on a pseudo-terminal and an OpenTelemetry collector on the machine itself, run through minutes of a fixed clock, with the events, dashboard frames, the operator’s tools run in other processes, and an account. Windows makes no pseudo-terminal, so there the thermometer’s readings are handed in and that line is the one that differs. Rebel’s process runs the services, poll waits on all of them at once, ipc is the control socket, serial reads the sensor, metrics, otlp and tracing export, log/files rotates the logs, and tui draws the dashboard.
