Invoicing for a small business: numbered invoices with VAT in exact decimals, drawn as PDF, mailed in the customer’s language, archived each year.

It keeps the company’s customers and items in SQLite, issues numbered invoices and credit notes with VAT computed per line and per country in exact decimals, and writes each document as a PDF drawn from a layout template, with the company’s logo and, on a euro invoice, the QR code a banking app scans to pay by SEPA transfer, and as an XLSX sheet beside it. It mails the PDF to the customer in the customer’s language, and at the year’s end exports the accountant’s CSV, reports the totals by VAT rate, and makes the year’s archive of every document, checked against its manifest.

The output on this page is its demo: a mail server the program carries, started on the machine itself, which checks each message and its PDF before taking it, and a month of a publisher’s invoicing under a fixed clock, with customers in Italy, France and the United States, invoices and a credit note, requests refused, every document mailed and a login refused, then the CSV, the totals and the archive. Rebel’s big keeps every amount exact, pdf, image and qr draw the documents, xlsx writes the sheets, i18n writes words, amounts and dates in the customer’s language, mail/smtp sends, and archive packs the year.
