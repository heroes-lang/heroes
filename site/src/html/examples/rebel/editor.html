A terminal text editor in the manner of nano and micro, which opens and saves files in any of the web’s standard encodings.

It reads and writes files in every encoding the WHATWG standard names, an encoding being the way a file’s characters are written as bytes: UTF-8 and UTF-16, windows-1252 and the ISO-8859 family, Shift_JIS, GB18030, Big5 and the rest, found from a byte order mark, guessed from the bytes or named, and saved back the way they came. The cursor moves by what a reader sees as one character, by word and by line, and wide characters are drawn as a terminal draws them. Undo takes back a word, a run of deletions or a whole replace at once; search and replace take regular expressions; a diff view shows the buffer against its file on disk; a file another program changes is offered for reload; and several files stand as tabs.

The output on this page is its demo: a scripted session played with no screen, on files it writes, printing what each step showed. Rebel’s tui and term draw the screen, unicode/segment moves the cursor and measures the text, unicode/encodings reads and writes every encoding, regex searches, diff compares, fs/watch notices a changed file, and containers/deque holds the history of undo.
