The maze solver, written again with Rebel: the shortest way from S to E, found and drawn.

Rebel’s strings reads the file’s rows, its sets holds the squares already reached, its lists turns the path round and its unicode writes a mark as text, each in place of a helper the original writes. The maze and its search stay this program’s own: a rectangle with one start and one goal, a position kept as one number, and the breadth-first search, which tries every square one step away before any square two steps away. The comment at the top of solve.hero says why Rebel’s own shortest-path function is not used instead.
