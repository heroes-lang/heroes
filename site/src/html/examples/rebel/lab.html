A scientific calculator and notebook: exact fractions and big numbers, complex numbers, matrices, statistics and plots, at a prompt or in cells.

Its language computes with exact whole numbers and fractions of any size, so 1/3 + 1/6 is 1/2, with floats and complex numbers, with vectors and matrices (an exact solve and inverse, determinants, the usual decompositions, eigenvalues, least squares), with statistics over series of data, random samples drawn from a seed, the integral and the root of a function, and plots written as PNG images. It runs as a REPL, a prompt that answers each line as it is typed, or over a notebook, a file of named cells where a cell runs after the cells whose names it reads, and after an edit only the cells the edit reaches run again.

The output on this page is its demo, run on the notebook shown under Input: the notebook’s report, its plots read back, edits and what each ran again, and a REPL session, the same on every run. Rebel’s big does the exact arithmetic, math/complex and math/linear the complex numbers and the small matrices, stats and random the statistics and the samples, graphs works out which cells depend on which, image/png writes the plots, and term is the REPL’s line editor.
