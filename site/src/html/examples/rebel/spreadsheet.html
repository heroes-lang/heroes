The spreadsheet evaluator, written again with Rebel: a file of cells and formulas in, every value or its error out.

Rebel’s numbers reads a cell’s row and the numbers a formula writes, its strings reads the file and pads the names, its unicode writes a column’s letter and its math keeps the highest row, each in place of the original’s own helpers, a C function bound to read decimal numbers among them. The formula language, its parser and the check that refuses a cell depending on itself through a chain of others stay this program’s own. It ends with status 1 when any cell failed, so a script can ask whether the sheet is sound without reading it.
