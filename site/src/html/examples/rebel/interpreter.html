The little language, scanned, parsed and run, written again with Rebel for the parts no language owns.

Rebel’s numbers reads the numbers a script writes and refuses one too large for 64 bits, its math does the script’s arithmetic and answers an error where the language’s own operators would stop the program, and its sets finds a parameter named twice. The language’s own parts stay this program’s: the scanner, which cuts a script into its tokens (the words and symbols it is made of), the parser and the evaluator. Rebel’s scanner reads the tokens of Go, and this language has escapes of its own and a line break that counts as a token, so its scanner is written here.
